Why
Route pricing already splits ride fare and airport. Booking persist folded both into final_fare_pence and left trips.airport_charge_pence = 0. Offer insert then called compute_driver_net_preview_from_gross(payable, airport=0), so airport was commissioned. Completion can add quote airport on top of an already-inclusive payable.

Before
  payable = ride + airport
  airport_charge_pence = 0
  commissionable = payable
  commission = round(payable * wave_rate / 100)
  driver_net = payable - commission

After
  payable stays the quote total (includes airport)
  airport_charge_pence = quote airport
  commissionable = payable - airport - other_non_commissionable
  commission = round(commissionable * wave_rate / 100)
  driver_net = commissionable - commission + airport + other pass-through

Wave rate is resolve_wave_commission_percent / resolveWaveCommission. No hardcoded airport amount or rate.

Edge path fails closed: a quote airport or route pass-through above zero must be written and read back before auto-dispatch.
